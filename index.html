About Kedai Tile Trip

Kedai Tile Trip is a free triple tile match puzzle set in Kedim's little kedai. Tap clay tiles from the pile into your tray of 7 slots. Three of the same tile clear away, and you win when the whole pile is gone before the tray fills up.

The tiles are Malaysian favourites: kuih lapis, onde-onde and karipap, wau kites, batik patterns, ketupat and pelita. Every new tile you match goes into your Kedai Collection album with a fun fact. The first 5 levels can't be lost, covered layers start at level 6 and locked tiles at level 15. Undo, Shuffle and Send back are always free, and there is an optional timed Challenge mode.

How to play

Tap a tile on the pile to move it into your tray of seven slots. Three of the same tile clear away. Clear the whole pile before the tray fills up. Darker tiles are covered, so free them by clearing the tiles on top, and padlocked tiles open after a set number of matches. Undo, Shuffle and Send back help when you are stuck.

Tips from Zaib

  1. Tap tiles on top first: darker tiles are covered and can't be picked yet.
  2. Try to keep two or three free slots in your tray before picking a new kind of tile.
  3. Stuck? Send 3 back moves tiles up to the shelf so your tray has room again.

Good to know

There are 36 levels. The first five cannot be lost, and the three helpers are free in every level: use none for 3 stars. Challenge mode adds a timer for each level. Your stars, best times and Kedai Collection are saved on your device. Sound effects only, no music.
